Case study · Tredence · 2026

Retiring a legacy store without guessing.

A supply-chain business is moving its product master data from a legacy MongoDB store to a new data platform. I'm one of two engineers on it. The old store can only be switched off once the business trusts the new one.

The problem

Counts lie

~10M product-attribute records with 100+ attributes. Matching row counts says nothing about whether a price, a unit or a category came across correctly. Manual checks took days.

The approach

Check every field

  1. Agree first. Requirement documents and stakeholder sign-off before the build, so "correct" is defined up front.
  2. Join on the key. Source and target joined on the business key in PySpark on Databricks.
  3. Compare field by field. Every attribute compared; each record labelled matched, mismatched, missing or duplicated.
  4. Re-run on every load. Checks run automatically, so the report is always current.
